Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how the detector works

How accurate is the detector? ▼

The models achieve approximately 85-90% accuracy on the test set, depending on the type of text. Performance is best on longer texts (3+ sentences) in English prose. Very short texts or non-English content may produce less reliable results.

What makes the Calibrated RF different from the regular RF? ▼

A regular Random Forest's probability estimates tend to be overconfident — clustering near 0% and 100%. The Calibrated RF applies isotonic regression to adjust these probabilities so they better reflect true likelihoods. This significantly reduces log loss while keeping accuracy similar, making the confidence scores more trustworthy.

Can it detect text from any AI model? ▼

The detector learns general linguistic patterns common to AI-generated text (consistent sentence length, specific vocabulary patterns, lower lexical diversity). It works best on the types of AI models represented in the training data. Newer or specialised models may produce text that this detector hasn't been trained on.

Why not use a transformer model for detection? ▼

This project prioritises explainability and accessibility. Transformer-based detectors are black boxes — they can't tell you why text was flagged. Our approach uses interpretable features and SHAP values so every prediction can be understood. It also runs on CPU without needing expensive GPU hardware.
